== Description ==

Run your CSA from your own website, and keep your season's money.

BoxDay fills your farm shares through your own site, lets members sort out their own swaps and away weeks, and hands you a harvest list and pickup sheets every week. You pay no monthly fee and no percentage of your sales.

Your members pay at your normal WooCommerce checkout, with the payment methods you already use. Their details stay on your site.

= Fill your shares =

* A sign-up page that works on a phone
* Full, half and add-on shares, weekly or every other week
* Sliding-scale prices, as many as you like
* Pickup sites and home delivery by ZIP code, with a limit for each
* Late joiners pay only for the boxes left
* A waitlist that offers freed places for you

= Make it easy to pay =

* Pay in full, or a deposit and up to 4 more payments
* No fees and no interest for your members
* Cards, checks or cash: take money however you do today
* Automatic reminders with a link to pay
* A clear list of who owes what

* Members swap items and skip weeks themselves, up to your cut-off
* No passwords to forget: each member gets a private link
* Skipped boxes can be donated, made up later or simply missed, your choice
* Members can move a pickup for one week or for good

* Plan each box in minutes, starting from last week
* See exactly what to harvest and how many boxes to pack

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What's a CSA? =

Community supported agriculture, or a farm share. A household pays a farm up front and collects a box of the harvest each week for a season. Some farms call it a veggie box or a produce box.

= Do I need WooCommerce? =

Yes. WooCommerce is free, and it handles your checkout, tax, receipts and payment methods. BoxDay does everything around it.

= Do I need WooCommerce Subscriptions? =

No. Payment plans work without it. Members get a link for each payment, so no card is stored and nobody is charged automatically.

= Do my members need passwords? =

No. Each member gets a private link by email. If they lose it, they can ask for a new one.

= Is there a limit on members? =

No. Free and Pro have no limits.

= Can I move from Farmigo, CSAware or a spreadsheet? =

Yes. Export your members to a spreadsheet and import them with their share and pickup site. Finish your current season first, then move for the new one.

= Can I take SNAP or EBT online? =

Not through BoxDay. If you take SNAP in person, you can add those members by hand.

= Does BoxDay handle the rules for my state? =

No, those are yours. BoxDay gives you tools that can help, such as a "Grown at" field for each item and, in Pro, box labels with your farm's name and address. Please check your own state's rules.

= Is the member agreement legal advice? =

No. It's a starting point for you to change. Have someone qualified check it.

= What happens if I stop paying for Pro? =

Everything keeps working. You stop getting new features and support.

= Which themes does it work with? =

BoxDay's pages sit in your own site and take your colors. We test on Storefront, Twenty Twenty-Five, Astra, Hello Elementor, OceanWP, Kadence and GeneratePress.
